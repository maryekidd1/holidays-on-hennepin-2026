Holidays on Hennepin: Decoration Catalog

Thank you for taking part in Holidays on Hennepin! We're excited to offer you a grant for decorations and craft supplies. Browse the catalog below, add what you like, and keep an eye on your remaining balance at the bottom of the screen. When you're ready, review and submit. Please place your order here, not on the retailers' websites. Our team will take care of the buying and ship your items directly to the address you provide.

Please submit your order by Friday, Nov 13. Orders after Nov 13 may delay your decoration day.

Questions about the catalog? Please contact us at the committee email and we will get back to you as soon as possible!
